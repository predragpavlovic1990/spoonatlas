Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy covers the Spoon Atlas website and the Spoon Atlas Publisher app (together, "Spoon Atlas", "we"). Spoon Atlas is run by an independent creator based in Serbia. Contact: spoonatlas@gmail.com.

Who the app is for

Spoon Atlas Publisher is used only by Spoon Atlas to publish its own videos to its own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It has no public sign-up and is not used to access anyone else's accounts.

What data the app uses

  • Account access tokens that YouTube (Google), Instagram (Meta) and TikTok issue when the Spoon Atlas account owner signs in. They are used only to upload Spoon Atlas videos and captions, to check that an upload finished, and to read basic profile information (account name and ID) needed to post.
  • Upload results, such as the ID and link of each published video, kept in a private log so failed uploads can be retried.

We do not collect data about viewers, followers or other users, and we do not read comments, messages or other people's content.

How data is stored and protected

Tokens are stored as private secrets in a private cloud workspace that only the account owner controls. They are never published, sold, shared or used for advertising. Access can be withdrawn at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Instagram under Settings, Apps and websites, TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. When access is withdrawn or the app is retired, stored tokens are deleted within 30 days.

Google API Services

Spoon Atlas Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The website

This website is a static page hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no tracking or analytics. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookbook purchases

Cookbooks are sold through Payhip and paid with PayPal. Payment and order data are handled by those services under their own privacy policies; Spoon Atlas receives only the order details needed to deliver the purchase.

Children

Spoon Atlas is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights and changes

You can ask what data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing spoonatlas@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
