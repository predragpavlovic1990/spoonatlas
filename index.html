About the Spoon Atlas Publisher app

Spoon Atlas Publisher is a small private tool used only by Spoon Atlas to upload its own finished recipe videos, with their captions, to the Spoon Atlas accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok through those platforms' official APIs. It is not offered to other users and does not read, collect or post anyone else's content. See the privacy policy and terms of service.
